What “local” means for privacy
These MCP tools do not save request or response content, enable telemetry, or call a model provider. They return selected evidence to your AI host. That host may retain it or send it to its model provider under its own policy.
OrcView reads logs already retained by the CLI; it does not make a second recording store. Its adapters cannot expose internals that the CLI did not log. Billed cost and provider latency remain unknown for imported CLI runs.
